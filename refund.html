Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refund or cancellation requests for course fees must be submitted to the institute with the student's name, course and payment reference.

Requests will be reviewed according to the course terms, applicable rules, and whether course services or learning access have already been provided. Processing time and any eligible refund amount will be communicated by the institute.

Before submitting this page to a payment provider, replace this general draft with the institute's actual refund and cancellation rules, including timelines and contact details.
