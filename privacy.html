Privacy Policy

We may collect information submitted through fee-related forms, such as student name, mobile number, admission identifier, course and payment amount, for student administration and payment support.

Use of information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, identify fee records, support payment processing and maintain accounting records. We do not intend to sell personal information.

Payment information

Payment processing, when enabled, will be handled by the selected payment provider. Do not submit card PIN, OTP or passwords through this website.

Retention and requests

Records may be retained for legitimate administrative, accounting and legal requirements. For requests regarding personal information, contact the institute using verified contact details published on the Contact page.

This policy must be reviewed and adapted to the institute's actual data practices before publication.
